# finger_training_app

Built by Alex Chen.

An app to help you train your finger strength (for rock climbing). It uses the methodology
discussed by Emil Abrahamsson in his Youtube videos.

The app's planned features:

- Built-in timers to help you with the routine
- Ability to modify the routine as you see fit
- Optional voice coaching so you don't have to look at your phone
- Optional notifications to remind you to train (choose how many times per day as goal)
- Habit tracking calendar (colours in the days you do it)
- Casual reminders to stretch or work on posture while you train
- Monthly check-ins where you can write down where you are currently at (to see gains)
- Useful tips you should know (could show while training)
  - Only train at 30-40% of max, you shouldn't be straining yourself
  - Train at most 10 minutes every 6 hours
  - Stretch or work on posture between training
- Not a "feature" but I want to add small banner ads (no audio)
